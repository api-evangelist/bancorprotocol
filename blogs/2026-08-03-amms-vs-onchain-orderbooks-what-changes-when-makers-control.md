TL;DR Traditional AMMs aggregate liquidity into shared pools, where a common curve determines how, and at what price, every position buys and sells. Traditional orderbooks give makers more control, usually expressing liquidity through separate fixed-price orders while relying on offchain matching or execution systems. Carbon DeFi introduces a different model: an onchain orderbook-style DEX where makers publish executable curves instead of being limited to fixed-price orders.
